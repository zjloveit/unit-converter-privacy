Unit Converter Explicit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Unit Converter Explicit ("the App") is a free unit conversion tool. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal data, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. It does not show ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

The App does not require an account. Conversion inputs are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include Google AdMob or other advertising SDKs.

Contact

See Support or contact the developer via the App Store listing.
