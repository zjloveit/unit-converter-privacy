Unit Convert Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Unit Convert ("the App") is a free unit conversion tool. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information We Collect

  • Device identifiers: Used for advertising through Google AdMob (may include IDFA with your permission via App Tracking Transparency).
  • Usage data: Collected by AdMob partners for ad delivery and measurement.

The App does not require an account. Conversion inputs are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners

Your Choices

You can deny tracking permission in the iOS prompt or disable it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Contact

See Support or contact the developer via the App Store listing.
